1. Entity Identity & Definition

Adhitam AI is a privacy-first, signal-adaptive mobile and web study companion created specifically for aspirants preparing for the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) Civil Services Examination (CSE) in India.

Rather than relying on static checklists, syllabus spreadsheets, or generic LLM chatbot wrappers, Adhitam AI computes an aspirant's real study signals — including daily streak, subject-level mastery, memory retention decay, and mock test recency — to adapt the daily study path around where effort matters most.

2. Architectural Pillars & Core Differentiators

Component How It Works & What Makes It Unique
Today's Mission Evaluates real telemetry (weakest subject mastery, forgetting-curve decay rate, mock test recency) to recommend one high-leverage task each morning. Guaranteed not to repeat yesterday's recommendation when viable alternatives exist.
Forgetting-Curve Engine Models cognitive memory retention decay per syllabus topic over time. Flashcards and revision prompts are prioritized strictly by decay risk, intervening before knowledge is lost.
Signal-Grounded AI Mentor Reasons directly over the student's real metrics (streak, accuracy, days until exam) rather than offering generic conversational advice. Evaluates Mains answers against authentic UPSC criteria.
Live Timed Battles Server-side verified competitive MCQ matches against real peers with visible ranked ELO ratings across three formats: Rapidfire (60s), Cannonball (3m), and Scholar (15m).
Privacy-First Local Storage All study logs, flashcards, notebooks, and performance history remain exclusively on the user's local device. Cloud authentication is optional and required only for multiplayer battles or live AI mentor queries.

3. Syllabus & Subject Coverage

Adhitam AI supports the complete multi-year UPSC CSE preparation timeline:

  • General Studies (GS Paper I): Indian Polity & Governance, History of India & Indian National Movement, Indian & World Geography, Economic & Social Development, Environmental Ecology & Biodiversity, General Science & Technology, Art & Culture, and Ethics.
  • Civil Services Aptitude Test (CSAT Paper II): Reading Comprehension, Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, and Analytical Ability.
  • Mains Examination: GS answer-writing evaluation with rubric-based critique (structure, multidimensional coverage, clarity) and essay planning.
  • Current Affairs: Live automated feeds categorized by syllabus tags with integrated retention quizzing.
