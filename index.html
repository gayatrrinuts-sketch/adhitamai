The core idea

Personalization that's actually earned, not guessed.

Adhitam AI doesn't ask what you think you're weak at. It watches what you actually do — every quiz, every flashcard, every day you skip — and reasons over that, out loud, in plain language.

A mission, not a checklist

Every day opens with one AI-picked focus — a weak-subject drill, a forgetting-risk rescue, or a live battle — chosen from your real signals, never the same two days running.

A forgetting-curve model

Every topic you've studied has a predicted retention score that decays over time. Adhitam AI surfaces exactly what's about to fade — before you lose it, not after.

A mentor with real memory

Ask the AI mentor what to focus on and it answers from your actual streak, accuracy, and days-to-exam — not a generic "study harder" script.

Live Battles

Prove it under a real clock, against a real opponent.

Ranked, timed MCQ battles against other aspirants — with a visible rating that only moves on merit. Grading always happens server-side against the verified question bank, so no one can game a result.

  • Rapidfire, Cannonball, and Scholar modes — 1 to 15 minutes
  • Unranked practice battles against a simulated opponent
  • A live leaderboard, always visible from the Prelims hub
