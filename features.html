Everything, in depth

Built around one question: what should you actually do today?

Every feature here feeds the same tracked profile of you — nothing is a standalone gimmick.

Today's Mission

One AI-picked focus, chosen from real signals — not a rotation.

Each morning, Adhitam AI scores every candidate action against what's actually true about your prep right now: your weakest subject's mastery score, the topic decaying fastest on the forgetting curve, how long since your last full mock, whether you're signed in for a battle. It picks whichever is genuinely most useful — and never repeats yesterday's pick if a real alternative exists.

  • A short, AI-written line explaining why today's mission was picked
  • One tap opens the real activity — a preset quiz, a flashcard set, a battle
  • Always resolves to something — current affairs is the guaranteed fallback
Forgetting-Curve Engine

Revision timed to when you're actually about to forget.

Every topic you touch gets a memory-strength value that decays predictably over time, the same underlying model behind spaced-repetition systems used for language learning — applied here to UPSC syllabus topics. Flashcards and revision nudges always surface the highest-risk topic first, so review time goes where it prevents the most loss.

  • A live predicted-retention score per topic, not a guess
  • Flashcards ranked by risk, not by subject order
  • Marking "Got it" feeds straight back into the model
AI Mentor

A study mentor that actually knows your numbers.

Ask what to focus on, get feedback on a written answer, or request a fresh practice set on your weakest topic — every response is grounded in your real streak, mastery scores, and days-to-exam, generated fresh each time rather than served from a script.

  • Answer & essay evaluation against real UPSC criteria
  • On-demand practice sets targeting your specific weak topics
  • A weekly strategy review, written in plain language
Privacy-First Data

Your prep history is yours. Literally — it's on your phone.

Profile, activity log, quiz history, notes, current-affairs entries: all of it lives in local on-device storage by default. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you deliberately turn on an optional online feature. Export a full backup file anytime, no account required.

  • Zero sign-in required for the entire study-tracking core
  • Export / import your data as a backup file, anytime
  • No ads, no data brokers, no selling your information — ever
